Who we are

Your vision is our blueprint; a flawless experience is our signature.

Visionary Events is a full-service event management and technical production company in Pune. We take ambitious ideas and run them as events, managing every detail so you can be present for the day instead of managing it.

That covers premier corporate events, social and private gatherings, cultural and entertainment festivals, large public and sporting events, and hybrid and virtual experiences.
