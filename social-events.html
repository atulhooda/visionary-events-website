Overview

Creating memorable moments for all occasions.

The best parties feel effortless to the people at them, and that takes planning. We take care of the theme, décor, entertainment, food and flow, and keep it all running quietly in the background.

Whether it is fifty close friends or a gala for hundreds, the evening is designed around the people you are celebrating.

How we work

From first call to final bow.

A clear four-step process, with one team accountable from the first brief to the last guest leaving.

01

Brief

We listen first: your goals, your guests, your budget and the feeling you want the event to have.

02

Plan & design

Concept, venue, vendors, layouts and a detailed run of show, shared with you at every step.

03

Produce

We build it: décor, staging, sound, light and screens, all tested before the first guest arrives.

04

Show day

Our crew runs the event on the ground so you can be present, and we handle the wrap-up after.
